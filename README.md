Entry for the make.fun competition
[Dad Joke Showdown](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mfnakmal-dad-joke-showdown-1d2ce3e5)
($5 pot, 3 paid places: 1st $2, 2nd $2, 3rd $1).

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes one line to `/output/entry.txt`: a dad joke
about a clock on the fridge.

> My dad stuck a clock on the fridge and called it quality thyme. I said that's not even a pun. He said, 'Give it thyme.'

Run it: `node main.ts` (writes `/output/entry.txt`, no network).
